# Welcome to Kakuro
Kakuro combines a crossword-shaped grid with number sums. Fill each cream cell with a digit from 1 to 9. Dark clue cells tell you which totals to make.

# Reading the clues
The number in the upper-right half of a clue cell gives the sum of the cells to its right. The number in the lower-left half gives the sum of the cells below it.

Follow each run until you reach another dark cell or the edge of the grid.

# The two rules
- The digits in a run must add up to its clue.
- A digit may appear only once within that run.

For example, a two-cell run totaling 10 can contain 1 and 9, 2 and 8, 3 and 7, or 4 and 6. It cannot contain 5 and 5. Crossing runs help you decide the order.

# Writing and erasing
Use the arrow keys to move the amber selector. Press 1-9 to enter a digit. Type another digit to replace it. Backspace, Delete or 0 clears the selected cell.

Rose cells contain given numbers. They are part of the puzzle and cannot be changed. Dark cells contain clues or separate the runs; you cannot write in them.

# Finishing a puzzle
The game checks the sums and the no-repeat rule after each entry. Any valid solution is accepted. When the board is solved, a congratulations panel appears.

Press Enter to view the completed board, or Escape to return to the journal. Completed boards remain locked.

# Returning to the journal
Press Escape during a puzzle to return to selection. Reopening the current puzzle keeps your entries. Choosing a different puzzle starts a fresh board.

Played and solved marks last for this session. Closing the application clears your entries and these marks.

# Choosing a puzzle
The journal contains 96 numbered puzzles and one extra puzzle, marked xxx. There are 24 cards per page. Each card shows its number and board size.

Use arrows to select a card and Enter to play. Page Up and Page Down, or [ and ], change pages. Home and End select the first and last card on a page.

Filled sakura blossoms show the selected puzzle's difficulty. More blossoms indicate a harder puzzle. Unrated puzzles are labelled UNRATED.

An outlined square marks a played puzzle; a checked square marks a solved puzzle. F1 opens this guide and F2 opens build information. Escape closes the game from the journal.

# A useful first step
Look for short runs and runs with only one empty cell. A two-cell clue of 3 must use 1 and 2; a two-cell clue of 17 must use 8 and 9. Use crossing runs to decide the order.

# Music and sound
Press F3 in the journal or during a puzzle to open sound settings. Use Up and Down to select Automatic, Off, AdLib, Sound Blaster FM or MPU-401 / General MIDI. Enter applies the device and plays a test sound; Escape closes the panel.

M toggles music and E toggles effects. B changes the Sound Blaster base port; P changes the MIDI port. Press Enter to apply port changes. Settings are kept in SOUND.CFG beside the game.

Automatic checks Sound Blaster FM, AdLib, then MPU-401. If no device responds, the game stays silent. Sound Blaster uses FM synthesis for these tracks and effects. MPU-401 needs a connected General MIDI synthesizer; an interface alone cannot produce sound.

# Reading these pages
Up and Down scroll one line. Page Up and Page Down move one screen. Space also moves down one screen. Home returns to the beginning; End goes to the last screen.

Press Escape or Enter to return to the journal.
